About

I am a researcher in natural language processing and large language models. My research focuses on understanding how language models represent and organize structured information, particularly entities, relations, and relational binding.

I am also interested in LLM interpretability, knowledge representation, personalized language models, and recommender systems. More broadly, I aim to understand the internal mechanisms of large language models and use these insights to build more effective and reliable NLP systems.

Research Interests

  • Large Language Models: representation, reasoning, and internal mechanisms
  • LLM Interpretability: understanding how entities, relations, and bindings are represented
  • Knowledge Representation: structured information and relational reasoning
  • Personalized Language Models: adapting models to users, preferences, and behavior
  • Recommender Systems: especially news recommendation and serendipity
